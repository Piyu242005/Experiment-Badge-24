Human organism

Overview

The human body is an organized biological system made of cells, tissues, organs and organ systems. These systems cooperate to maintain internal balance, obtain energy, exchange gases, move materials and respond to the environment.

Cells → tissues → organs → organ systems
Different systems continuously depend on one another
Homeostasis helps keep internal conditions stable
