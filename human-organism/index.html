🧬 Human Organism

A simple visual introduction to how the major systems of the human body work together to keep us alive, active, and healthy.

What is a human organism?

A human organism is a living biological system made of cells, tissues, organs, and organ systems. These parts work together to perform essential functions such as breathing, circulation, digestion, movement, growth, and protection.

Major Body Systems

🧠

Nervous System

Controls communication, sensation, movement, memory, and responses. It includes the brain, spinal cord, and nerves.

❤️

Circulatory System

The heart and blood vessels transport oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and waste products around the body.

🫁

Respiratory System

Brings oxygen into the body and removes carbon dioxide. The lungs are its main organs.

🍎

Digestive System

Breaks food into nutrients that the body can absorb and use for energy, growth, and repair.

🦴

Skeletal System

Provides structure and protection. Bones also help the body move and store important minerals.

💪

Muscular System

Muscles produce movement, maintain posture, and generate body heat.

🛡️

Immune System

Protects the body from harmful pathogens and helps identify and remove threats.

🧪

Endocrine System

Produces hormones that regulate processes such as metabolism, growth, stress, and reproduction.

🚰

Urinary System

Filters the blood, removes certain wastes, and helps maintain water and electrolyte balance.

👶

Reproductive System

Contains organs involved in reproduction and the production of reproductive cells and hormones.

🔬 Key Idea

  • Cells form tissues.
  • Tissues form organs.
  • Organs work together in systems.
  • Body systems depend on each other.
  • Homeostasis keeps internal conditions balanced.
  • The goal is coordinated survival and function.
